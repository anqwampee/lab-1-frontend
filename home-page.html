Our mission

AltShop was created for those who don't want to dress like everyone else. We offer unconventional clothing that lets you express your individuality and inner world through style - because what you wear speaks louder than words.

  1. Build a catalog of clothing from independent and alternative designers that stands out from the mass market.
  2. Provide a simple and convenient product browsing and ordering experience.
  3. Create a space for feedback where customers can share their impressions and suggestions.
  4. Guarantee the quality and authenticity of every product listed.
  5. Grow a community of people who value self-expression through fashion.
